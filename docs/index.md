# Mind Weaver — Landing

> **an AI team of experts at your command**

## The problem

AI coding tools are fast. But one model, one shot, one mistake. When the AI hallucinates, you only find out after the code is written, reviewed and merged.

## The promise

Mind Weaver sits between your idea and your code. It makes your AI models talk to each other, disagree, and surface the issues you would have noticed too late.

## What you can do today

1. Open any file in VS Code.
2. Run `> Mind Weaver: Validate with AI experts`.
3. Get a side-by-side critique from the AI providers you trust.

No web automation. No lock-in. Your keys, your data.
